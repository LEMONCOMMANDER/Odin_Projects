Gormet Sourdough Pizza

This unbelievable pizza will leave you wanting more. It's delicate crust will have a beautiful crumb and bubble, and the carmelization will make the experience chew and crisp all at once. Each bit will leave you desperate for the next!

Ingredients

  • water
  • sourdough starter
  • salt
  • sauce
  • cheese

Steps

  1. make dough
  2. put dough in oven for 5-6 min
  3. top with a light layer of cheese. Bake pizza for 2-3 minutes longer
  4. sauce and cheese pizza. Cheese should drape over the edge of the crust to apply proper carmelization
  5. bake another 10-12 min checking for doneness.
